# 005 - Marketing And Pricing Pages

**Status:** Todo · **Depends on:** 001 · **Spec:** [spec.md](../spec.md#public-content)

**Summary** - Publish the primary product, pricing, guide, and comparison pages
with a consistent self-serve sandbox call to action.

**Acceptance criteria**

- [ ] Given a public visitor, when the homepage or guide pages are read, then the
  product, intended customer, core workflow, and sandbox CTA are clear.
- [ ] Given a visitor before signup, when pricing is requested, then public plan
  information is available without a sales form.
- [ ] Given comparison pages, when viewed, then claims are specific, dated, and
  do not imply unsupported features.
- [ ] Given a mobile or keyboard user, when navigating public pages, then primary
  content and CTA remain usable.

**Tasks**

- [ ] Build homepage and public pricing.
- [ ] Build SOC 2 guide, evidence checklist, and comparison pages.
- [ ] Link API/MCP documentation and packet example.
- [ ] Add responsive/accessibility browser smoke tests.
